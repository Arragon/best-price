# Reporting

Lead with the decision boundary: budget, use, hard constraints, research scope, and capture time.
Then separate:

1. Model-level trade-offs and why each model remains in the pool.
2. Listing-level Primary, Extra, and Review candidates.
3. Comparable used range and exact new quotes, only where evidence supports them.
4. Risks, missing facts, partial coverage, failed sources, and stopping reason.

For each candidate give price, bundle/condition evidence, seller facts without inference, source
link, observation time, deviation from the profile, and the next human check. Do not rank solely by
a composite score, and do not present Review items as verified deals.
