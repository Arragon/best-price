# Evaluation policy

Determine eligibility before statistics, then score only when evidence is adequate. Keep product
model value separate from a specific listing's suitability. An evaluation may be `null`.

Use direct title/description/platform-field evidence for listing type, functional defects,
bundle, condition, marketing signals, and contradictions. Preserve the excerpt and field path.
Invalid evidence or model failure leaves the fact unknown and must not remove the raw listing.

Unknown seller data is neutral. A very low price triggers verification; it is not itself a scam
label. Confirmed deposits, rentals, wanted ads, accessories, and incompatible bundles do not enter
normal same-SKU price samples. Major unresolved safety or functional risk cannot be cancelled by
a high price score.
